HIPAA Privacy & Confidentiality Policy

Walden Bailey Chiropractic is committed to protecting the privacy and security of all Protected Health Information (PHI) in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and the HITECH Act.

1. Definitions

Protected Health Information (PHI) means any individually identifiable health information, including demographic data, that relates to a patient's past, present, or future health condition, provision of health care, or payment for care.

2. Staff Obligations

  • Access PHI only as necessary to perform assigned job duties.
  • Never share patient information with unauthorized individuals.
  • Log out of all systems when leaving workstations unattended.
  • Use only practice-approved devices and software to access PHI.
  • Immediately report any suspected or actual breach of PHI to the Privacy Officer.

3. Electronic Systems

SpinePay Pro is the authorized practice management system. All data entered into SpinePay Pro is considered PHI and is protected under HIPAA. Staff must not copy, transfer, or export patient data to unauthorized systems or personal devices.

4. Minimum Necessary Standard

When using or disclosing PHI, staff must make reasonable efforts to limit the information to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose.

5. Breach Notification

Any actual or suspected breach of unsecured PHI must be reported to the Privacy Officer within 24 hours of discovery. Failure to report a breach is itself a HIPAA violation.

6. Penalties for Non-Compliance

Violations of HIPAA may result in civil and criminal penalties ranging from $100 to $50,000 per violation, up to $1.9 million per year for identical violations. Criminal penalties include imprisonment. Additionally, violations may result in immediate termination of employment.

7. Acknowledgement

By signing below, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to comply with this HIPAA Confidentiality Policy. This agreement remains in effect for the duration of your employment and indefinitely thereafter with respect to PHI accessed during your employment.
